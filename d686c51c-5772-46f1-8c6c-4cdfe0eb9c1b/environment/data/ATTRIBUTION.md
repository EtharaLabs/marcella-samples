# Attribution

The pixel data are cutouts of TESS full-frame images, retrieved with TESScut (Brasseur et al. 2019, astrocut) from
the Mikulski Archive for Space Telescopes (MAST). TESS data at MAST are in the public domain
(https://archive.stsci.edu/publishing/data-use). This dataset includes data collected with the TESS mission,
obtained from the MAST data archive at the Space Telescope Science Institute (STScI). Funding for the TESS mission is
provided by the NASA Explorer Program. STScI is operated by the Association of Universities for Research in
Astronomy, Inc., under NASA contract NAS5-26555.

The star sample comes from Hattori, Angus, Foreman-Mackey, Lu and Colman 2025, AJ 170, 15
(https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/add0ab), published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence.

Changes: the stars are identified only by opaque identifiers; the cutouts were binned to 2 hours and reduced to the
arrays described in COLUMNS.md, and every header, coordinate and catalogue identifier was removed.
