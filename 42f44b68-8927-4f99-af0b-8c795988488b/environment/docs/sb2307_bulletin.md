# Service Bulletin SB-2307: HP-450 compensator and valve plate

Issued by the pump manufacturer on 2023-05-15 and adopted by the mine's maintenance planning on 2023-06-12.

## Description

SB-2307 replaces the compensator spool, compensator spring and valve plate of the HP-450 main hydraulic pump with
kit 450-2307. The work takes about three hours and is done with the pump in place on the truck during a scheduled
service, or on the bench before a pump is fitted.

## Compliance at this mine

- Each truck has a campaign week in the rollout plan below. The pump fitted to a truck is modified at the truck's
  first 1,000 h service in or after its campaign week.
- After that service, an unmodified pump fitted to the truck is modified on the bench before fitting.
- A pump that is already modified is not modified again, whichever truck it is fitted to.
- The modification is recorded as task SB2307 on the work order of the service, or on the installation work order
  of a pump modified before fitting. The serial master's sb_2307 flag is then set to Y.
