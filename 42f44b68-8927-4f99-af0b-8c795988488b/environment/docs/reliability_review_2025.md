# Reliability review: HP-450 main hydraulic pump

R. Mbeki, Reliability Engineering, 2025-04-22. Records: MIS export to 2025-03-31, 339 pumps, 167 confirmed failures.

## Findings

- The HP-450 life distribution is Weibull with shape 2.01 and scale 18,396 h; B10 is 6,006 h.
- Pumps with SB-2307 embodied have a confirmed-failure hazard 0.17 times that of unmodified pumps (95 percent
  interval 0.12 to 0.24), a reduction of about 83 percent.
- The fleet should expect 21.5 confirmed failures in the 26 weeks after 2025-03-31. A stock of 15 pumps covers the
  26 weeks with probability 0.95.

## Recommendations

1. Keep SB-2307 as the fleet standard and complete it on every pump returned to stores.
2. Hold 15 HP-450 pumps in stores across the three sites.
3. Repeat the review with the full SB-2307 rollout once two more years of records are available.
